privacy policy

We don't want your data, and we mostly never see it.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This site has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. The software runs on your machine with your credentials — your Notion token and workspace never reach us.

01 — the siteWhat this website collects: nothing of its own

The LifeOS website is a set of static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers or advertising, and has no login. We do not build a profile of you, and there's no database on our side to put one in.

in short

No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. The site itself stores nothing about you.

02 — hostingTwo things load from other companies

To be fully honest, two ordinary web mechanics do involve third parties:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs to deliver the page and guard against abuse. This is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which can see your IP address. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. We use it only to load fonts — no analytics.

We don't control these providers and receive nothing from them about you. If you'd rather avoid the font request entirely, the site remains fully readable with its fallback system fonts.

03 — the softwareYour token and workspace stay with you

LifeOS is software you run yourself. When it provisions a Notion workspace, it uses a Notion integration token that you supply and that lives in your environment. That token, and the contents of your Notion workspace, are sent directly from your machine to Notion's API. They never pass through us — we operate no servers in that path and have no way to see them.

in short

Your Notion token and data go from your computer straight to Notion. We're not in the middle.

04 — notionThe one service the software talks to

Because LifeOS provisions into Notion, using it means sending data to Notion under Notion's own privacy policy. Your relationship there is directly with Notion, not with us.

05 — childrenNot directed at children

The Project is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

06 — changesIf this policy changes

If the site ever starts doing something new with data, this policy will be updated first and the "last updated" date above will change. The history is public in the Git repository.

07 — contactQuestions

Ask by opening an issue on GitHub.
